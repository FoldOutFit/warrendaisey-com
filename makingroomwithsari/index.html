Meet Sari

She helped leaders grow through change. Then life asked her to do it herself.

Each loss took something away, and each one showed her what was worth keeping.

For 20 years, Sari Daisey helped leaders grow through change, most recently as a Chief Learning Officer. Then life asked her to do it herself.

She was diagnosed with breast cancer twice. She went through a divorce after 27 years of marriage. She lost her job. Each loss took something away, and each one showed her what was worth keeping. Along the way, she earned a second-degree black belt, training side by side with her sons.

Today Sari is the founder of Making Room with Sari. She helps people stop waiting for a crisis to decide what matters and start making room for it now.

  • Former Chief Learning Officer
  • 16 years at IBM leading global learning and collaboration strategy
  • M.S. in Human Resource Management, Widener University
  • Diagnosed with breast cancer twice
  • Second-degree black belt, earned training alongside her sons
  • Based in Wilmington, Delaware
