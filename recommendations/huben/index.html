Letter of recommendation

From Michael T. Huben

Assistant Professor, Department of Marketing and Professional Sales · Commonwealth University – Bloomsburg

March 11th, 2026

Recommendation for Warren Daisey:

One privilege afforded to a university instructor is the opportunity to submit letters of recommendation for students. As a faculty member, you have the opportunity to witness students grow and thrive in the university environment over their academic careers. Over the last year or so, I have had the opportunity to work with Warren Daisey as an advisor to the coed business fraternity Pi Sigma Epsilon. Warren made an immediate positive impact on the fraternity with his energy and his willingness to jump in and actively participate in all activities.

An example of his willingness to stretch himself was his participation in the Keystone Sales Challenge, held in October of 2025. Warren agreed to participate and ended up as one of the strongest competitors in the contest, advancing only to miss the finals by the slimmest of margins. He demonstrated a tremendous work ethic and collaborated with others to ensure that he was well prepared for his presentations.

Warren is a true leader, as evidenced by his participation and leadership roles in numerous clubs across campus. He truly cares about the university, as demonstrated by his enthusiastic participation in all activities. As a former sales manager for more than twenty years, I found that one trait that consistently separated some sales professionals from others was the intangible quality called impact. Warren has that intangible quality; he makes a positive impact on everyone on day one. He will be an asset to any student government position and will make decisions in the best interests of our university. He has my highest recommendation.

Michael T. Huben

Michael T. Huben
Assistant Professor, Department of Marketing and Professional Sales
Commonwealth University – Bloomsburg
