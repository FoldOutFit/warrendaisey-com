Letter of recommendation

From Jeffery L. Osgood

President · Commonwealth University

I don't write recommendations like this often, but Warren Daisey is the kind of student who makes you want to. Warren has presence. He walks into a room and you feel it. He is curious, driven, and unapologetically ambitious. He wants to learn everything, do everything, and be part of everything — and that intensity is not about ego. It is about purpose. What matters most to me is this: Warren's ambition is never just for himself. He wants to win, yes — but he also wants the people around him, the project he is working on, and the institution he represents to win too. He cares deeply about Commonwealth University and carries that pride with him in how he shows up, how he speaks, and how he works. Warren is at his best when the stakes are high. He leans in, not back. He asks the harder questions, takes responsibility, and expects a lot — first and foremost from himself. That combination of confidence, work ethic, and loyalty is rare, and it is powerful. I have no doubt that Warren will make an impact wherever he goes next. He will lead, he will contribute, and he will leave places better than he found them. Any organization that brings him on will quickly understand why I believe so strongly in his future.

Jeffery L. Osgood

Jeffery L. Osgood, Jr. PhD MPA
President
Commonwealth University
