Her story

Each loss showed her what was worth keeping.

For 20 years, Sari Daisey helped leaders grow through change, most recently as a Chief Learning Officer. Then life asked her to do it herself.

She was diagnosed with breast cancer twice. She went through a divorce after 27 years of marriage. She lost her job. Each loss took something away, and each one showed her what was worth keeping. Along the way, she earned a second-degree black belt, training side by side with her sons.

Today Sari is the founder of Making Room with Sari. She helps people stop waiting for a crisis to decide what matters and start making room for it now. Audiences walk away with honest hope, practical next steps, and the belief that it’s not too late to change course.

Resilience When Life Doesn’t Turn Out the Way You Planned

Diagnosis, divorce, job loss. Sari shares what she learned walking through all three, and how to recover, reset, and lead again when the plan falls apart. Audiences leave with hope they can use and one practical step to take right away.
