Introduction

About

Warren Daisey is a student at Commonwealth University–Bloomsburg pursuing a bachelor’s in Biomedical Science and Political Science, with a Pre-Law concentration and minors in Professional Sales and Black Studies. His goal is a career in biomedical sales, bringing science, communication, and strategy together.

Driven and competitive, he enjoys building relationships, solving problems, and creating value through clear communication and strategic thinking. He does his best work in fast-paced environments, and cares about professional growth, technical sales, and results — with an interest in building long-term partnerships.
